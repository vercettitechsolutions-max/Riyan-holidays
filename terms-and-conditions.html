4. How a booking works

  1. You choose a property, your check-in/check-out dates, number of guests, and any optional add-ons on our website, and receive a live price quote.
  2. Submitting the booking creates a provisional booking with a unique booking reference, and holds those dates for you while you complete the next step.
  3. You then provide your name, WhatsApp number, email address, and a valid government ID proof on the guest details page.
  4. Once your guest details are received, our team confirms your booking and follows up with you (currently via WhatsApp, and in future also via our online payment gateway) to collect the required advance payment.
  5. A booking is treated as confirmed only once we have received your guest details, valid ID proof, and the required advance payment as set out in Section 5.

We reserve the right to decline or cancel any booking, at any stage prior to full confirmation, where dates become unavailable, information provided is incomplete or appears fraudulent, or we are otherwise unable to honour the booking - in which case any amount already paid will be refunded in full.

5. Pricing and payment

  • Prices shown on our website are per stay (for the dates and number of guests you select) in Indian Rupees (₹), and include the room total plus any optional add-ons you choose.
  • Riyan Holidays is not currently registered under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) Act; accordingly, no GST is charged on bookings at this time. This will be updated here if that changes.
  • Prices are confirmed only once a booking is completed; displayed quotes may change before that if not acted upon, due to seasonal pricing or availability changes.
  • An advance payment of 25% of the total booking amount is required to confirm your booking, with the balance payable before or at check-in, as communicated to you at the time of booking. Until our online payment gateway (Razorpay) is live, this advance is coordinated manually with our team over WhatsApp.
  • Once online payments are enabled, payments are processed securely through Razorpay; we do not receive or store your card, UPI, or net-banking credentials.
