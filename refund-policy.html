Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short
  • Cancel 7 or more days before check-in and get a full refund.
  • Cancel 3-6 days before check-in and get a 50% refund.
  • Cancelling within 48 hours of check-in, or not showing up, is non-refundable.
  • If we have to cancel your booking - including due to weather or a landslide beyond our control - you always get a full refund or a free reschedule, your choice.

1. Overview

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains what happens if you need to cancel or change a confirmed booking with Riyan Holidays, and how we handle cancellations on our end. It forms part of, and should be read together with, our Terms & Conditions. All timeframes below are measured against your booking's check-in date and time.

2. Cancellation by you

If you cancel a confirmed booking, the amount refunded depends on how far in advance you cancel:

When you cancel Refund
7 or more days before check-in 100% of the amount paid
3 to 6 days before check-in 50% of the amount paid
0 to 2 days before check-in No refund

To cancel, message us on WhatsApp or email with your booking reference (see "How to request a cancellation" below) - the cancellation takes effect from the date and time we receive your request, not the date you decide to cancel.

3. Date changes

We understand plans change. If you'd like to move your stay to different dates instead of cancelling, we allow one free date change per booking, provided you request it at least 3 days before your original check-in date and your new dates are available. The new dates are subject to the pricing applicable to those dates, and any difference in price will be collected or refunded accordingly. Requests made within 3 days of check-in are treated as a cancellation under Section 2 above.

4. No-shows and early check-out

If you do not arrive by the end of your scheduled check-in day without notifying us, your booking will be treated as a no-show and is non-refundable. Similarly, if you check out earlier than your booked dates, we are unable to refund the unused portion of your stay, as the property is held exclusively for you for the full period booked.

5. Cancellation by Riyan Holidays

On rare occasions we may need to cancel a confirmed booking - for example, due to an unforeseen maintenance issue, a genuine double-booking error, or circumstances described in Section 6 below. If this happens, we will notify you as soon as possible and you will receive, at your choice, either a 100% refund of all amounts paid, or a free reschedule to alternative dates or (where available) a comparable property.

6. Weather and events beyond our control

Kodaikanal can occasionally be affected by extreme weather, landslides, road closures, or government-imposed restrictions. If such an event beyond our reasonable control makes your stay impossible or unsafe, you will receive, at your choice, either a full refund or a free reschedule to alternative dates, even if this falls within a normally non-refundable window under Section 2. This does not apply to a guest's personal travel disruptions (such as a cancelled flight or train) that are unrelated to the property or its location, which are handled under the standard cancellation terms in Section 2.

7. Add-ons

Optional add-ons (such as a fireplace or pickup service) booked along with your stay follow the same refund timeline as your room booking under Section 2, provided the add-on has not already been arranged or delivered at the time of cancellation. An add-on that has already been arranged specially for your stay (for example, a pickup already scheduled) is non-refundable once arranged.

8. How and when refunds are paid

Since advance payments are made directly to our business UPI ID or bank account (we don't have an online payment gateway), approved refunds are paid back to you by UPI or bank transfer, to the account or UPI ID you tell us to use, within 7-10 business days of the refund being approved.

9. How to request a cancellation or refund

To cancel a booking or ask about a refund, please contact us with your booking reference number through either of these channels:

  • WhatsApp: +91 89032 00612
  • Email: riyanholidayskkl@gmail.com

We will confirm receipt of your request and the applicable refund amount within 48 hours.

10. Contact us

If you're not satisfied with how a cancellation or refund request has been handled, you can reach our Grievance Officer, Prakash, Property Manager, using the same WhatsApp number or email above, or refer to the grievance redressal process in our Terms & Conditions.
